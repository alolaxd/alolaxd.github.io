About me

I'm a developer who enjoys poking at how things work under the hood. Lately that's meant virtual machines, emulation and GPU acceleration on Apple silicon through my AndroiOS project, plus smaller macOS utilities built with SwiftUI.

My toolkit spans high-level scripting and low-level systems work. I write Python when I want to get something done fast, Swift and C when I care about control, and I'm slowly getting more comfortable in Rust.
